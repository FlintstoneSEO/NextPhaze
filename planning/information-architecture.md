# NextPhaze Information Architecture

## Approved routes

| Route | Purpose | Primary action |
|---|---|---|
| `/` | Broad Nashville training landing page | Book Training |
| `/training/` | Compare training options | Book Training |
| `/training/one-on-one/` | Explain focused $60 coaching | Book Training |
| `/training/group/` | Explain $30 group training | Book Training |
| `/training/speed-agility/` | Explain movement-development focus | Book Training |
| `/training/wide-receiver/` | Explain receiver and football skill work | Book Training |
| `/coach-carrington/` | Establish verified coach credibility | Book Training |
| `/book-training/` | Route users into the approved booking workflow | Complete booking or inquiry |
| `/privacy/` | Describe actual data handling once known | None |

Service-area pages and Terms are deferred until verified local logistics and policies exist.

## Navigation

Primary: Training, Pricing, Coach Carrington, Service Area, Book Training.

Pricing and Service Area link to descriptive homepage sections until unique route purposes are verified. Mobile navigation uses a native button and disclosure panel. Book Training remains the only visually dominant action.

## Conversion paths

- General visitor: Home to training choice to Book Training.
- Private-training visitor: One-on-one page to Book Training.
- Group visitor: Group page to Book Training.
- Football visitor: Wide Receiver page to Coach proof to Book Training.
- Coach-research visitor: Coach Carrington page to training options to Book Training.

## Internal-link rules

Every training detail page links to Training, Book Training, and at least one related training type. The coach page links back to service pages. No route is orphaned. No duplicate Nashville or neighborhood doorway pages will be created.
