# NextPhaze Industry Brief

## Expected user information

Nashville sports-performance sites commonly explain training focus, athlete fit, coach credibility, location, pricing or packages, and a direct consultation or booking path. NextPhaze should meet those expectations while avoiding unverified claims about measurement systems, facilities, outcomes, or age programs.

## Reference categories

1. Local speed and performance facilities: Six1Five and DiamondFit establish expectations around movement mechanics, speed, agility, and progression.
2. Established training brands: D1 demonstrates how coach credentials can support a service offer without replacing it.
3. Position-specific football programs and marketplaces: QB Country and Athletes Untapped validate route running, receiver training, private coaching, and Nashville-specific search intent.

## Differentiation opportunity

Lead with direct coach access, simple $30 and $60 price anchors, a wider development offer than receiver training alone, and documented championship-level credibility. Present these as reasons to evaluate NextPhaze, not unsupported superiority claims.

## Trust barriers

- What does a session actually focus on?
- Is group or one-on-one the right fit?
- Where does training happen?
- Who is the coach and are the credentials verifiable?
- What does it cost and how does booking work?

## Prohibited copying targets

Do not reproduce competitor composition, language, illustrations, photography, navigation sequence, or program naming. Do not imitate assessment dashboards or data-driven claims without an actual measurement practice.
